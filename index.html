Research foundation

Key references used to shape the prototype's logic and safety notes.

FAO — Manual for fruits & vegetablesPackaging should account for respiratory rate, gas exchange, cooling/wetting, strength and water loss.
FAO — Modified atmospheresExplains passive/active MAP and the relationship between product respiration and package permeability.
FDA — Food types & conditions of useFood-contact compliance depends on food type and conditions such as ambient, refrigerated, frozen and heat processing.
FDA — Food-contact material statusComponents of food-contact articles need an applicable regulatory basis for the intended use.
ASTM — Packaging / permeation standardsProvides standardized test methods for oxygen transmission, water-vapor transmission and gas permeability.
FDA — Food packaging safetyFood-contact substances and migration are part of packaging safety evaluation.
Prototype disclaimer: This application is a hackathon decision-support prototype, not a certified packaging specification. OTR/WVTR, thickness, MAP gas composition and shelf-life values must be validated experimentally for the actual food + package + process combination and checked against the destination market's food-contact regulations.
